# Pollinator Feeding Notes (Miracle-Gro)

General-purpose feeding ratio: mix 1 tablespoon of Miracle-Gro for every
gallon of water, for flowering plants and vegetables.

For pollinator gardens:

- Use sparingly — too much nitrogen reduces blooms.
- Apply only during active growth (spring/summer).
- Avoid feeding during drought or extreme heat.
- Never apply to legumes (clover, Baptisia, Amorpha) — they fix their own
  nitrogen and don't need synthetic feeding.
