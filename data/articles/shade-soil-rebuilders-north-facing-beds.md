# Shade Soil Rebuilders — Legumes for North-Facing Beds

North-facing beds in Zone 9a receive cooler temperatures, lower light, and
higher moisture retention. These conditions favor fungal-dominant soils and
shade-tolerant nitrogen fixers.

## Best shade-friendly nitrogen fixers

- Baptisia australis (False Indigo)
- Amorpha fruticosa (Indigo Bush)
- Alsike Clover
- Erythrina herbacea (Coral Bean)

## Why these plants work

- They fix nitrogen without synthetic fertilizer.
- They thrive in partial shade.
- They build deep root systems that stabilize soil.
- They increase organic matter in low-light beds.
- They support pollinators even in shade.

## Seasonal timing

- Plant in spring or fall.
- Avoid summer planting in shade (fungal pressure).
