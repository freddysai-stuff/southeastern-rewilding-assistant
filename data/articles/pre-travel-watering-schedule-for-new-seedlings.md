# Pre-Travel Watering Schedule for Freshly-Seeded Lawns and Beds

A practical protocol for leaving newly-broadcast seed (lawn-scale, via
automated sprinkler zones) unattended during an extended trip, without
losing the establishment window. Complements
`drip-irrigation-timer-schedule-for-seed-germination` (bed-scale drip) and
`dry-weather-irrigation-for-cover-crop-establishment` (general dry-spell
watering) - this one is specifically for the "no one will be home to
adjust the timers" scenario.

## While still home (establishment window)

Keep the automated, alternating 3-times-a-day sprinkler schedule running
exactly as-is right up until departure - this is the schedule that gets
tiny grain and clover seedlings through their first, most fragile days
(zero heat tolerance, easily crushed by foot traffic). Avoid walking on the
treated area entirely during this period.

## The day before departure

Switch timers over to a **morning-only, ~35-minute deep-soak** schedule.
By this point roots are established enough to dive deep into the soil
(reaching down into old chemical hardpan layers, in a recovery-project
context) rather than needing frequent shallow watering, and a single deep
morning soak trains them to keep growing downward while unattended.

## Before walking out the door

- Put fresh batteries in the timers.
- Confirm the dial/settings are on the morning-only deep-soak schedule, not
  the frequent germination schedule.
- Mow any recovering permanent turf patches (e.g. Centipede) down flat
  before leaving so they stay tidy without maintenance during the trip.

## Why this works

A single, infrequent deep soak (as opposed to continuing frequent light
watering) avoids two failure modes while away: shallow, heat-vulnerable
roots from over-frequent light watering, and completely drying out from
under-watering with no one home to adjust for an unexpected hot spell. It
mirrors the same germination-to-establishment timer logic used for bed-scale
drip irrigation, just tuned for whole-lawn sprinkler zones and an
unattended multi-day absence.
