# Soil Behavior in Brunswick, GA (Zone 9a)

Brunswick's coastal sand is:

- Fast-draining
- Low in organic matter
- High in heat retention
- Low in nutrient-holding capacity

This means:

- Clover thrives.
- Compost is essential.
- Organic teas activate biology quickly.
- Water evaporates rapidly.
- Soil temperatures can exceed 110°F in summer.

## Best soil-building strategies

- Clover broadcast
- Compost topdressing
- Kelp + alfalfa teas
- Shade-friendly legumes in north-facing beds
- Avoid synthetic fertilizers (they leach instantly)
