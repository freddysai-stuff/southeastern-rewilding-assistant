# Two-Year Aggressive De-Compaction Cover Crop Plan (30+ Year Chem-Lawn Recovery)

A more aggressive alternative to the standard rotation in
`multi-year-chemlawn-recovery-transition-plan`, aimed specifically at lawns
subjected to 30+ years of continuous synthetic chemical treatment, where
soil is severely compacted and biologically dead. Broadcast freshly charged
biochar (see `biochar-charging-tea-compost-worm-castings` and
`diy-biochar-production-tlud-weber-grill`) directly onto the ground first,
lightly rake it into the top inch, then immediately sow the seed blends
below.

**Reconciliation note:** the existing multi-year plan's Phase 2 uses a
gentler main-lawn blend (Browntop Millet + Alyceclover). This plan swaps in
Sorghum-Sudangrass + Daikon Radish for Year 1 summer instead - a visually
much larger, more disruptive combo chosen specifically for deep mechanical
fracturing of decades-old compaction. Either blend is valid; choose the
milder millet/legume mix for a lower-visibility transition, or this blend
when maximum root-driven de-compaction (and neighbor-baffling height) is
the priority.

## Year 1 — Deep fracturing & massive biomass

**Summer blend: Sorghum-Sudangrass + Daikon Radish**
- Sorghum-Sudangrass grows 8-12 feet tall, pumping large amounts of carbon
  into dead soil through an extensive root system while creating a visually
  dramatic wall of green.
- Daikon Radish ("Tillage Radish") drives an 18-inch taproot straight down,
  acting as a biological jackhammer through the compacted "plow pan" left by
  years of mowing and chemical treatment.

**Winter blend: Crimson Clover + Hairy Vetch**
- In late fall, chop down the summer crops and leave the green debris in
  place as mulch, then sow directly into the residue.
- Both are nitrogen fixers that form a dense carpet; Crimson Clover wakes up
  in spring with bright red blooms, Hairy Vetch with purple flowers -
  together they pull nitrogen from the air and lock it into the soil for
  free over winter.

## Year 2 — Microbial explosion & pollinator activity

**Summer blend: Buckwheat + Sunflowers**
- Buckwheat matures in about 30 days, suppresses lingering weeds fast, and
  solubilizes soil phosphorus, making it available to later plantings.
- Standard foraging Sunflowers pair well for pollinator activity and visual
  chaos that reads as the opposite of a sterile chemical lawn.

**Winter blend: Winter Rye + Austrian Winter Peas**
- Winter Rye builds topsoil structure fast via a massive fibrous root
  system and exudes natural compounds that suppress weed seed germination.
- Austrian Winter Peas add a nitrogen-fixing legume partner to round out
  the blend before spring transition.

## Flower beds vs. lawn application

Flower beds generally need less structural disruption than a full lawn
transformation - dig charged biochar directly into planting holes or till
into the top 4-6 inches rather than surface broadcasting. This reduces
watering needs significantly through the hot summer months once the
biochar is holding moisture.
