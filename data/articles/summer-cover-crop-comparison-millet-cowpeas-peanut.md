# Low-Profile Summer Cover Crop Options for Sandy Southeast Soils

For hot, sandy, drought-prone Southeast soils, several summer cover crops
combine aggressive soil-building with a naturally low or mower-tolerant
growth habit — useful where county rules cap lawn height (commonly ~12").

## Comparison

| Cover Crop | Max Height | How to Keep it Low-Profile | Major Soil Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pearl / Browntop Millet | 4-5 ft | Mow weekly at 6-8" — handles aggressive mowing well | Massive root network breaks up compacted sand; heavy carbon input |
| Cowpeas (Iron & Clay variety) | 12-18 in | Naturally vines/crawls; only mow runaway tendrils | Heavy nitrogen fixing from dead sand |
| Perennial Peanut (Arachis glabrata) | 6-8 in | No mowing needed — naturally short, dense carpet | Permanent nitrogen-fixing groundcover, zero fertilizer needed |

## Browntop / Pearl Millet details

- Drought-tolerant summer grass; loves hot sand.
- Broadcast seed at **1-2 lb per 1,000 sq ft** (10-12 lb for a 6,000 sq ft
  lot); lightly rake/drag to bury seed ~1/2" deep.
- Germinates in 4-5 days in warm weather.
- Buy in bulk 50 lb farm-supply bags rather than small retail packets —
  significantly cheaper, and leftover seed stores for years in a sealed
  container for next season's planting (no need to rebuy).
- Do not rely on it to self-seed for next year if you mow it green each fall
  — it won't have set enough viable seed.

## Alyceclover (Alysicarpus vaginalis) — millet's lawn partner

Not a true clover but a spreading legume with small oval leaves. Pairs well
with millet in a "stealth lawn": tolerates mowing at 6-8" without going
stemmy, forms a dense mat underneath the millet stalks, and fixes nitrogen
continuously. A better mower-compatible partner than upright legumes like
Partridge Pea, which must flower/set seed to persist and dies if mowed
before blooming.

## Sourcing tip

Buy cover crop seed in bulk 50 lb bags from farm-supply/tractor-supply
stores rather than small specialty packets — often cheaper even when you
only need a fraction of the bag, and the surplus keeps for next season.
