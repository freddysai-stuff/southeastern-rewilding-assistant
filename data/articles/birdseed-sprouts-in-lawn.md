# Birdseed Sprouts in Lawn — Identification & Management

Birdseed often contains millet, sorghum, cracked corn, and wheat, all of
which germinate readily when they hit moist soil.

## How to identify birdseed sprouts

- Tall, upright, light-green stalks
- Broad blades (millet/sorghum)
- Rapid vertical growth
- Appears in clusters where seed fell

## Are they harmful?

No — they are harmless temporary cover crops.

## Should you remove them?

Only if they interfere with your lawn plan. Otherwise, they:

- Add organic matter
- Shade soil
- Reduce erosion
- Feed wildlife

## How to remove

- Mow normally
- Hand-pull if desired
- Overseed clover to outcompete them
