# Barometer Bush (Texas Sage) — Pruning & Care

Texas Sage (Leucophyllum frutescens) is a drought-tolerant shrub that blooms
after humidity spikes, earning it the nickname "barometer bush."

## Pruning rules

- Prune late winter–early spring, just before new growth begins.
- Remove no more than one-third of the plant at a time.
- Avoid pruning during bloom cycles.
- Shape lightly — heavy pruning reduces flowering.

## Soil & water

- Thrives in sandy soils.
- Requires excellent drainage.
- Overwatering causes root rot.
