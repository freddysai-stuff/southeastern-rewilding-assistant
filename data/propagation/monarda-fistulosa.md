# Propagating Wild Bergamot (Monarda fistulosa) by Division

1. Divide established clumps every 2-3 years in early spring or fall.
2. Dig up the clump and split rhizomes into sections with at least one growth
   node each.
3. Replant divisions 12-18 in. apart in part-shade to full-sun locations.
4. Water well until established; division helps control mildew-prone
   overcrowded clumps.
