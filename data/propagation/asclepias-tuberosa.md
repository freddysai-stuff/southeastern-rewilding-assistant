# Propagating Butterfly Weed (Asclepias tuberosa)

1. Collect seed pods once they split open and turn brown/tan (late summer/fall).
2. Cold-moist stratify seeds in the refrigerator for 30 days, wrapped in a damp
   paper towel inside a sealed bag.
3. Sow indoors 8-10 weeks before last frost, or direct-sow outdoors in fall.
4. Taproot forms quickly — transplant seedlings while still young to avoid
   root disturbance.
5. Expect first bloom in year two.
